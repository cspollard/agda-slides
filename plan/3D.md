# 3D: what remains

`Slides.Trig` and `Slides.Three.{Point,View,Solid,Render}` exist: points in cylindrical or
Cartesian form, a view as two exact rotations (`viewT`: a rational tangent of the half angle gives
an exactly orthogonal turn, no trig), sine and cosine as exact folding plus a Horner polynomial
of a chosen order (`Slides.Trig`; order 5 is within 3·10⁻⁴), solids as faces with a material
(fill, opacity, rim), and `render : View → List Solid → List (Dia ℕ)`, which drops back faces of
closed solids and paints by mean depth.  It compiles to `Dia`, so every backend, the layout laws
and the animations work unchanged; nothing in `Slides.Diagram.*` knows about it.  `ATLAS.agda` is
the only user.

## Next
1. `pitch` (the third rotation), `box`, `cylinder`, and one example that is not a detector, such
   as a cube with labelled faces for a tensor slide.  If it needs something the first user did
   not, that is the design feedback this step exists for.
2. Optional: perspective (division by `d − z`; orthographic stays the default); an `Anim` hook (a
   view is a few numbers, so a rotation over a slide sequence is a natural animation parameter);
   revealing solids one by one.
3. If no second picture is wanted soon, stop after step 1's example and leave solids as they are.

## Limits, deliberately
- Painter's order by mean depth is wrong for interpenetrating or large non-convex faces.  That is
  accepted for concentric shells, boxes and cells; a caller can order solids explicitly.  No
  hidden-line removal or BSP.
- No `Float`: output is reproducible and `--safe` holds.
- The polynomial's accuracy is not proved (the library has no reals); it is checked on closed
  values by `make test-numeric`.  Proved: `cos 0 = 1`, `sin 0 = 0`, the identity turn acts as the
  identity, a flat view moves nothing.  Open: orthogonality of `tangent` (c² + s² = 1) and
  invariance of `cis` under full turns; both need ℚ lemmas that the standard library lacks.

## Open questions
- Depth ordering API: per face within a solid and per solid as given today; revisit after the
  second example.
- Whether `Dia` should be over a coordinate type other than ℕ if a second front end (plots,
  graphs) also wants rationals; it would touch the layout laws, so it is its own plan.
- Precision: `viewT` and the order parameter settle it today; a `snap` to a fixed denominator
  was not needed because Haskell integers absorb the growth.
